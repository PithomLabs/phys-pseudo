## 1. Definite correction 1 — the operator-domain requirement was wrong

Confirmed: the §2.2 assumption audit of `plan/plan2.1.md` listed
`a|0⟩ ∈ dom(a)`. The lowering argument requires two different
memberships, neither correctly stated there:

- `a|0⟩` must be **defined**: `|0⟩ ∈ dom(a)` — the original text wrote
  `a|0⟩ ∈ dom(a)`, wrong vector on the wrong side.
- `H(a|0⟩)` must be **defined**: `a|0⟩ ∈ dom(H)` — named correctly in
  `plan2.1.md` §1 but dropped from the audit list.
- The commutator identity `H·a|0⟩ − a·H|0⟩ = [H,a]|0⟩` must hold on the
  state: all required operator compositions defined (`|0⟩ ∈ dom(H)`,
  `a|0⟩ ∈ dom(H)` for the left-hand composition; `|0⟩ ∈ dom(a)` for
  `a|0⟩` to exist at all).
- `H|0⟩ ∈ dom(a)`, so `a·H|0⟩` is defined. This one is **derived, not
  independent**: `H|0⟩ = E₀|0⟩` and `|0⟩ ∈ dom(a)` with `dom(a)` a linear
  subspace imply `E₀|0⟩ ∈ dom(a)`. The implication is recorded as such —
  not as an independently satisfied assumption — and its own premises
  (linearity of `dom(a)`; the eigenstate relation holding within the
  domain) remain part of the unresolved `?`, since the fixture establishes
  neither.

**Meta-rule (binding on the repair):** these conditions stay marked
unresolved (`?`) unless the fixture establishes them; they must never
appear in the assumption audit as satisfied premises. The `plan2.1.md`
audit sentence violated exactly this — recording an unestablished (and
misstated) condition as a listed assumption — the same defect class the
reviewer found in the original fixture (annotation inventing a premise),
repeated one level up in the correction itself. Recorded here as such.

## 2. Clarification 2 — what `spectrum` establishes

The recurrence `Eₙ₊₁ = Eₙ + ħω` establishes *the energy sequence
generated by raising the ground state*: `Eₙ = E₀ + n·ħω` for the tower
`{|0⟩, a†|0⟩, …}`. "This is the entire spectrum" additionally needs:

- each raised state is an eigenstate (raising argument applied per level —
  same domain-condition class: `|n⟩ ∈ dom(a†)`, `a†|n⟩ ∈ dom(H)`);
- the tower **exhausts** the assumed discrete spectrum — the lowering
  argument on any relevant eigenstate (not just `|0⟩`), with its domain
  requirements, showing no eigenvalue lies outside the sequence.

The fixture does not fully establish these. Two required statements:

- The expected findings must explicitly say `spectrum`'s conclusion
  **retains an unresolved domain/completeness dependency**.
- **`GOLDEN` means the encoded mathematical steps were independently
  checked — not that every conclusion in the fixture is unconditionally
  established.** This distinction is carried in the fixture's status line
  itself.
