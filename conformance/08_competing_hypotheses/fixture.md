# Fixture 08 — Competing hypotheses (symbolic toy)

- **Status:** TOY (golden by construction)
- **Mode:** exploration
- **Constructs exercised:** `given`, `define`, `assume`, `claim`, `if/then/else`, `?`
- **Invariant test:** I2 (comparison ≠ verdict; neither claim promoted or demoted by structure)
- **Document conformance (`PSEUDO.md`):** non-conforming, preserved verbatim
  — `derive discriminant` states no explicit result (§4); the `assume` is
  unnamed (§2, though never cited); `s` is used without declaration (§2);
  the `?` sits at the same indentation as the `else:` line, so it attaches
  to nothing (§6) — it concerns the conditional as a whole and would need
  to be at column 0.

## Document

```text
given data: an observed residual pattern r over a declared sample space
define H₁: r is generated by process P₁ with parameter set θ₁
define H₂: r is generated by process P₂ with parameter set θ₂
assume the sample space and the residual definition are fixed for both hypotheses
claim C₁: H₁ accounts for r
claim C₂: H₂ accounts for r
derive discriminant from [H₁, H₂, data] | the two differ in predicted sign of a secondary statistic s(r)
if sign(s) > 0:
    then C₁ remains consistent with r while C₂ is in tension with r under these assumptions
    else: C₂ remains consistent with r while C₁ is in tension under these assumptions
    ? [neither branch is evaluated here: s(r) has not been computed; 'tension' is conditional on the fixed definitions, not a refutation]
```

## Expected MRC findings (recognition-level)

1. `C₁` and `C₂` are **peer claims** — both bare, both unevaluated; neither
   inherits a favored status from being written first, second, or from the
   shared premises.
2. `discriminant` is a `derive` whose result is a *difference in prediction*
   — a comparison, not a selection. Recognizing this prevents the classic
   upgrade: discriminant ⇒ winner.
3. The branch content is stated as *consistency vs. tension under stated
   assumptions*, never as true/false. The `?` pins the two open facts: the
   statistic is uncomputed, and tension is conditional on the fixed
   **assumptions** (the unnamed `assume` line — the earlier wording said
   "definitions", Stage 3).
4. **I2 both directions:** forbidden to infer that the taken branch
   *establishes* its hypothesis, and forbidden to infer that the other
   branch *refutes* its hypothesis — "in tension under these assumptions"
   with uncomputed `s(r)` is a conditional, reversible statement.
5. *Transfer rule — about hypothetical downstream documents, not a
   property of this fixture (Stage 3 relabel):* if a downstream step ever
   reports `s(r)`'s sign, that datum in the ancestry does not convert
   tension into refutation (ancestry datum ≠ support, plan2 I2 restated):
   measurement-status and support-status are not document-derivable.
6. **(Stage 3 BLOCKING ×2 — added)** two gaps the fixture under-
   anticipates: (a) `discriminant`'s result — the sign *difference*
   between H₁ and H₂ — appears in none of `[H₁, H₂, data]`; (b) both
   branch verdicts additionally require an undeclared proposition ("H₁
   predicts s > 0; H₂ predicts s ≤ 0", or similar) — without it the
   `then`/`else` content does not follow from `discriminant` at all. The
   two-way split of the three-valued predicate `sign(s)` also leaves
   s = 0 unhandled.

## Rationale

Tests the plan requirement that conditional reasoning and competing
hypotheses remain expressible without treating conjectures as failed
proofs, and that a discriminating derivation does not by itself select.

## Negative cases

**N1 — premature winner (ANTI-PATTERN):** `claim C₁: PROVEN, since H₁ was
not excluded by the discriminant.` Must be flagged twice: status smuggling
(I3) and non-exclusion ≠ establishment (I2).

**N2 — asymmetry (ANTI-PATTERN):** writing `assume H₁` while leaving `H₂`
a claim. Must be flagged: smuggling one competitor into premise-status
changes the comparison's structure silently.
